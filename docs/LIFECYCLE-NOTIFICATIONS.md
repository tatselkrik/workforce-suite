# Payroll appraisal and learning notifications

Workforce HR, Workforce Payroll and Workforce Employee show scoped notices from
protected server projections. Notice state is separate from workflow decisions.
Reading a notice never approves a request, changes pay or updates a learning record.

## Payroll notices

Published payslips and advice produce personal notices. Individual payment
recording is a separate event: a batch status cannot establish another person's
receipt. Own notices omit amounts, bank references, private notes, evidence,
tax identifiers and source bundles. Recording a final settlement does not describe
one of its contained sources as another cash payment.

## Development reminders

Assigned current reviewers receive appraisal deadlines. Staff with protected
Development administration can see submitted reviews awaiting publication. Own
published-review notices contain no rating or private notes.

Training start and due dates, and certificate expiry reminders, follow current
own, reporting and HR scope. Completion, cancellation, reassignment and archiving
remove obsolete tasks. A recorded certificate date does not establish accreditation.

## Read state and access

Lifecycle read markers remain distinct from compatible earlier notice keys.
Surface-specific markers use caller-derived identity. A staff member using
Workforce Employee still receives only their own notices. Mixed marker writes
are transactional and do not alter canonical records or immutable history.

Native notice links open the corresponding permitted record. An informational
notice must not promise a detail view that its application cannot open. Current
role, reporting and employment checks apply on each refresh and read-state change.
