# Review checklist — leave policies and openings0.1.49

Candidate checklist. Delivery/install status is recorded separately in the
verification file. Use fictional employees for balance-changing checks.

1. **Leave > Opening balances > Enter opening.** Select an active employee and a
   finite type. Enter an available balance after HRIS reservations and the unspent
   expiring carry inside it. Enter a private reason/reference and review the source
   request declaration. Preview shows before/after amounts and retained requests.
2. Changing a value removes its old preview. Fractional quarter days, negative
   targets, expired/excess carry, a duplicate yearly opening and unresolved
   later-year reservations are rejected. Zero and exact half days are supported.
3. **Post reviewed opening** as an Administrator. Availability matches the target;
   the original rate/booking/carry basis stays in History. Repeated delivery of the
   same request does not grant it twice. Later corrections use Credit adjustments.
4. **Import CSV.** Save the six-column template. Use existing employee/type codes
   shown in the app. Review every row; wrong codes, duplicate employee/type rows
   and invalid quantities are flagged. One bad row blocks the entire import.
   Error/review CSV export preserves existing files and rechecks protected access.
5. Preview a valid CSV and post all rows as Administrator. All targets appear in
   History together; a stale employee/policy/credit/request basis requires review
   of the entire batch again. An exact retry creates no duplicate openings.
6. **Leave policies > Schedule policy / approval-chain version.** Enter the effective
   date and reason. Calendar, labels and approvals may start today or later.
   Grant/accrual/carry changes and new finite types start on a future January1.
   Earlier filed requests retain their original labels, dates, charges and plan.
7. Expand policy history. Only an unactivated future tail without active request
   references can be withdrawn. A recorded future credit cannot lose its type;
   offset its dated effect first. The version/withdrawal history remains visible.
8. Change an employee's future allowance default after a basis was captured. The
   known current-year grant and incoming carry stay unchanged. A current-year
   entitlement change needs an audited credit. Later same-day credits apply once.
9. Test HR Generalist and Manager views. HR can preview/read private history but
   cannot post. Managers see only own/current-team minimum openings, with no HR
   reasons, references or author audit. Ordinary Employees use their own portal.
10. The existing local portal shows its own updated balance. Cross-version request
    spans require splitting on older portal builds. Earlier unverified phone/email
    checks remain deferred; this checklist does not claim they were performed.

Attendance calendars, holiday-sensitive shifts, exit named-approver dependencies
and payroll's separate holiday-classification review use the affected dates.
No financial rate, issued slip/receipt, external message, paid service, public
portal or biometric connection is introduced by this release.
