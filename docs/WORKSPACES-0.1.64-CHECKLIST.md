# Focused workspaces0.1.64 — testing checklist

0.1.64 is installed and privately published. Work/scheduler are paused.
See [delivery evidence and verification limits](WORKSPACES-0.1.64-DELIVERY.md).

- Administrator: open Settings > Workspaces. All six optional sections start shown.
- Hide/show Hiring, Development, Organization chart, Exit & clearance, Payroll &
  payslips or Reports, save, then refresh another desktop session.
- Hidden sections leave existing records, roles and portal access intact. Core
  Overview, attendance/leave, Profile, notices and authorized administration remain.
- Recruiter lands in Hiring; Learning & Performance Officer enters Development;
  Payroll Officer enters Payroll. Combined duties retain their combined workspaces.
- HR/Managers retain Employees with their allowed Directory/Organization/Development
  sections. Development still contains Goals, Appraisals, Training, Certifications,
  Career plans and authorized Review cycles in one workspace.
- Other staff see their personal attendance/leave/payslips and assigned hiring or
  clearance tasks. Account Manager and company controls remain Administrator-only.
- A second Administrator change keeps your settings choices for review. Confirmed
  rejection permits cancellation/review; uncertain delivery retries the same change.
- Hide a section while another person has a draft open: they can finish/cancel it.
  Afterward navigation returns to an available workspace without discarding work.
- At compact size/125percent/dark, the selected sidebar item remains visible.
  Manual sidebar scrolling continues to work.
- Company logo, Profile, settings/preferences and signed updater remain available.

Ordinary Employees remain portal-only. Payroll separation and later HR-function
features are not part of this increment. Confirmation-email/physical-device/agency
acceptance and paid-service work retain their existing deferrals.
