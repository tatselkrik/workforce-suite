# Employee services —0.1.67 review checklist

In HRIS, open **Employee services** from the sidebar and choose **Load requests**.
In the separate local employee portal0.1.8, choose **Services**. Use fictional
requests for testing; all request messages are shared with the requester.

- Create a document request, information-update request, benefits question or
  general support request. HR can choose a company employee; other staff and
  the portal submit for themselves. Creating a request changes no employee/pay fact.
- Open a request to see its requester, assigned handler, due date, status and
  complete shared history. Search/filter the native queue without changing records.
- As HR, route to an active staff handler, change the optional due date or return
  it to the HR queue. A Manager or specialist sees only own and specifically
  assigned requests, rather than every team member's services.
- Reply and progress work through In progress, Waiting on employee and Completed.
  Give a shared outcome. The requester can reply/cancel active own work; only HR
  reopens completed/cancelled work. Original messages and cancellations stay saved.
- In the portal, submit a request, open its history, reply and cancel with a reason.
  Staff accounts still see only their own requests here, without routing controls.
- Leave a draft open and try navigation, refresh or sign-out. Save/discard it first.
  An interrupted save retains the same frozen request and Retry same save control.
  A confirmed rejection needs a fresh view and explicit current-version review.
- Check compact/light/dark layouts and the lower save controls. Confidential
  employee-relations cases remain under Employees and do not appear in Services.

This checklist is for product review, not proof of official document generation,
external delivery, benefits enrollment, automatic personnel edits or payroll changes.
Portal access remains loopback-only; do not publish, tunnel or expose it to a LAN.
