# Annual compensation tax reconciliation

Open **Payroll → Annual tax**. HR/Admin can prepare a per-person year-end or termination close, review its paid sources, add evidenced current-employer openings and successive prior-employer certificates, submit it and have another Administrator approve immutable tax advice. Managers receive only their own issued advice. An Administrator records the actual settlement separately. See versioned release evidence for hosted/install state.

## Calculation and dates

The [BIR RR11-2018 annualization procedure and2023-onwards schedule](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/local/pdf/RR%20No.%2011-2018.pdf), printedpp33–35, were checked24October2026. Annual taxable brackets are250,000/400,000/800,000/2million/8million; excess rates15/20/25/30/35percent, with base tax0/22,500/102,500/402,500/2,202,500. Calculations use integer centavos and half-up rounding once per annual tax, rather than multiplying a rounded monthly figure.

Recorded actual payment dates determine included income in this calendar year. Source includes paid monthly/cutoff/13th slips and payment revisions. Approved but unrecorded pay must be resolved first. Paid periods outside this year's service interval can contribute received income without covering missing service days. Year-end uses31December; a termination close has separate service-end and payment-as-of dates. It does not change employment or access. Drafts can be prepared in advance; submission/approval requires completed2026 payment facts. Future years require a new rule review.

Missing current-employment intervals require evidenced openings, including explicitly reviewed zero-income periods. Openings cannot overlap included paid coverage. Taxable opening compensation is already net of mandatory employee contributions and includes any taxable shared benefits. Prior-employer amounts come from same-year certified2316 figures, with withholding **net of any previous refund**. They cover successive employment before the current hire; concurrent employers need separate review. The app does not infer no prior employer from an empty list: HR must confirm that review.

## Shared benefits and adjustments

Recompute a single annual90,000 exclusion across current and previous employers'13th-month/qualifying other benefits. Remove the taxable benefit portions already in the source before applying the combined annual benefit result; income must not be added twice. A documented reclassification can move qualifying benefits already in regular taxable figures into that cap. It cannot exceed still-included regular taxable compensation. Existing13th-month external/shared-benefit floors must reconcile. Additional mandatory contributions recorded on current13th slips are deducted once; contributions already removed from salary/opening/certified taxable figures are not deducted again. Do not assume all allowances qualify as shared-cap benefits or apply a blanket minimum-wage exemption to additional taxable compensation.

Annual due minus original withholding and earlier **settled** annual adjustments produces a signed difference: positive additional withholding or negative refund. Pending earlier advice blocks another issue. Later changed sources require a new reviewed adjustment; issued slips are never rewritten. Available last-pay cash is an explicitly reviewed settlement input. An excess deficiency is shown separately as employer advance/unfunded withholding, without creating an automatic employee debt or wage recovery.

## Privacy and workflow

HR-only drafts/source/certificates/notes are protected by current memberships and RLS. Issued own advice includes aggregate calculations, not former-employer names, evidence, source bundles or private notes. Revisions and fresh source checks apply before saving, submission and independent approval. Company payroll locking prevents concurrent advice from double-adjusting the same facts. Changed sources require refresh and all three reviews again. Invalid saved inputs reset to a safe unreviewed draft with earlier facts retained in audit; open native form edits remain available for correction.

This increment reconciles **recorded paid compensation** and issues closing advice; it does not inject withholding into an unpaid last payslip. HR must apply/settle it with the employer's payroll within BIR deadlines. RR11-2018 requires deficiency withholding from last compensation, employer handling when insufficient, and refunds by25January of the following year or with last compensation upon earlier termination. The app records settlements, not bank transfers, refunds sent, government filings, remittances, official2316 certificates, substituted-filing eligibility or an official alphalist. In-year cumulative-average supplementary withholding and automatic last-pay application remain separate work. Fringe-benefit tax, concurrent employers and unusual residency/tax regimes need separate professional review.
