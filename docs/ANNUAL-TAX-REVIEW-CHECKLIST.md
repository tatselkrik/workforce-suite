# Annual tax — review checklist

1. Open Payroll → Annual tax. Create a year-end draft or completed termination close, choose the employee and review service-end/payment-as-of dates. Creating a close must not archive or disable that person.
2. Open the worksheet. Confirm included paid salary/cutoff/13th sources and any outstanding-payment blockers. Enter evidenced missing current-employer intervals; overlapping intervals must be rejected.
3. Add successive prior-employer2316 totals, including shared benefits and net withholding after refunds. Review benefit classification without adding income twice. Confirm all three reviews and save.
4. Inspect the automatic annual taxable, tax due, withholding credits, additional withholding/refund and any deficiency beyond available last-pay cash. An incomplete review must block submission.
5. Submit, then sign in as another Administrator. View saved private evidence and approve. The submitter must not approve their own close.
6. Check issued advice and PDF/CSV. Former-employer labels, evidence and private notes must stay out of the receipt. A Manager should see only their own advice.
7. Record an actual settlement date/reference. This records history and must not imply money was transferred. A later amendment must credit the settled adjustment; old receipts remain unchanged.
