# Payroll Desktop0.1.0 and HRIS0.1.65 — testing checklist

Candidate checklist; exact installation/delivery is recorded separately. Both apps
use the same company and account. Payroll needs Administrator or Payroll Officer
responsibility; assign additional responsibilities only through HRIS Account Manager.

- Open Payroll Desktop from its Windows shortcut; verify version0.1.0/company logo,
  then sign in using the existing HRIS account. Enter submits the sign-in form.
- Check the scrolling sidebar: Monthly registers, Salaries & benefits, Payroll
  holidays, Payslips, Reports, Statutory rules, Cutoffs,13th-month pay, Annual tax,
  Payments, Final pay, Wage compliance, Amendments, Government and Data exchange.
- Open saved records/history and compare their totals to the existing prototype.
  Existing issued slips, individual received dates, remittances, linked corrections,
  sources, reports, local CSV/Excel exchange and PDF advice remain available.
- Try a draft salary/register edit with a synthetic employee. Save/refresh; source
  changes still require review, and independent approval remains a different actor.
  Payment/remittance recording retains its existing restrictions and moves no money.
- Under Government > Reconciliation, choose agency/month to view remittances.
  Registration, internal preparation and the existing BIR draft/review tools remain.
  Agency acceptance and deferred template-dependent adapters are not newly claimed.
- Check the Profile photo, readonly Employment fields, own contacts/emergency
  contact, account/security and sessions. Settings holds shared display preferences.
  Payroll account/profile/password/session changes affect the same person's account.
- Open HRIS0.1.65: financial administration opens through Open Payroll Desktop.
  My payslips retains only your own published advice, including for Administrators.
  Company settings and Account Manager stay in HRIS. Ordinary Employees use the
  separate local portal; unrelated staff roles cannot sign into Payroll.
- At compact size/125percent, scroll the Payroll sidebar and select Government,
  Data exchange and Settings. Selected rows should remain visible. Company optional
  HRIS visibility flags do not revoke Payroll responsibility or hide its independent
  financial sections. Drafts remain protected during refresh/navigation.

The first Payroll package is installed locally and has its own app identity and
version. Its automatic update channel is not configured. HRIS's existing private
updater remains separate. Install the local Payroll companion on each PC before
using the newly separated financial screens. No payroll data migration or rate
change occurs in this extraction.
