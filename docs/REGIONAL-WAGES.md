# Regional wage reviews

Payroll → Wage compliance compares the recorded daily basic salary, or the exact
365/366-day equivalent of a true monthly basic salary, with an evidenced regional
eight-hour daily basic floor. It never changes salary, tax status or canonical
employment. Account roles and the number of accounts in this prototype do not
determine legal coverage, industry or establishment size.

The first pack is verified24October2026 and bounded through31December2026.
Its regional/category/date sources are retained in the catalog. Unsupported
historical days, nonstandard coverage and ambiguous classification remain explicit
unresolved results; they never become a zero floor or a compliant result.
Published future tranches activate only on their effective date. A newer issuance
requires a refreshed pack and HR review of current official sources.

HR/Admin record effective dated employee classification, actual workplace and
private evidence for sector, establishment size and applicability. A different
Administrator approves it. Approved values remain immutable; retirement/cancellation
retains history. Retire a superseded classification and enter reviewed nonoverlapping
replacement intervals. A changed saved work location/employment type invalidates the
classification for new checks; archiving alone does not invalidate owed pay.

A documented exemption requires independent review and specific evidence; it is
shown separately from a rate meeting the recorded floor. Unknown/nonstandard
coverage is not an exemption. Allowances, OT and night premiums are not used to
hide a below-floor basic rate. A rate shortfall is a reference-rate gap, not an
automatic arrears calculation. Actual paid days, mid-period salary changes, wage
distortion, equal pay, trainees, piece rates and domestic/government employment
need separate review. Missing attendance never creates a deduction.

Administrator-only optional enforcement is off until the company deliberately
enables it with a reason. When enabled, each monthly/cutoff submission and approval
requires resolved recorded-rate checks for every included employee. Immutable HR-only
snapshots preserve classification, rate pack and salary facts, including when
enforcement is off. Changes after submission require return/review/resubmission.
The server trigger also applies to older installed clients; issued slips are unchanged.
This check is not a certificate that every wage obligation has been satisfied.
