# Workforce Suite User Guide

This guide explains everyday work in Workforce HR, Workforce Payroll and Workforce
Employee for Android. Your company supplies the applications and accounts. The
screens available to you depend on your assigned responsibilities and company settings.

## Getting started

| Application | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| Workforce HR | Employee records, hiring, attendance and leave administration, Development, employee support and HR reports |
| Workforce Payroll | Salary setup, pay preparation, independent approval, payslips, payment records and payroll reporting |
| Workforce Employee for Android | Your own attendance, leave, payslips, service requests, profile and notifications |

### Sign in

1. Open the application supplied for your company.
2. Enter your company account email and password.
3. Press Enter on desktop or select **Sign in**. Complete any account-confirmation requirement communicated by your administrator.
4. Review the company name and your profile before recording work.

You do not need to choose a database or enter a company connection. Contact your
administrator if the company branding or account is incorrect. Ordinary Employee
accounts use the Android application; desktop access requires staff responsibilities.

Each app signs in independently. Authentication stays in memory. Save your changes
before signing out or closing an app. After process termination, sign in again.

### Find your work

Start with **Overview** in Workforce HR. **Needs your review** opens leave,
attendance, overtime and daily report reviews assigned to your current scope.
Choose a category to narrow the queue. **Review next** opens the next eligible
item in that category. Use **Back to review queue** to keep your place. Save or
discard an open decision before switching.

Press **Ctrl+F** to focus search in the Directory, daily attendance roster,
Daily reports or Leave list.

Use the sidebar to browse workspaces and the circular profile control for your
personal details.

In **Employees → Directory**, use **Previous** and **Next** to browse larger
results. Select an employee's name to open their record.

In **Notifications → Document expiry**, filter **Overdue**, **Due today** or
**Next 30 days**. **Open record** shows the exact document, even if titles are
repeated. **Back to Notifications** returns to the filtered list.

Workforce Payroll has its own financial navigation. Staff using Workforce Employee
receive the same own-record view as any other employee.

## Employee records and accounts

### Open or update an employee

1. In Workforce HR, use **Find employee** or press **Ctrl+K**. Search by name or employee code.
2. Select a result, or press **Enter** when one result matches. You can also open the record from **Employees**.
3. Review **Employment**, contact details and emergency contact information. Record department, job title, joining date, employment type, location and **Reports to** where known.
4. Save the changes. If another user changed the record, refresh and review the latest values before saving again.

From a search result, **Back to search results** keeps your query. Close or save
the record to resume your previous workspace with its filters in place.

Use **Attendance**, **Leave** or **Learning & performance** from a saved employee
record to open that workspace with the employee selected. Save or close open edits
before switching.

Employment history retains earlier saved values. Correct an error by saving the
correct information; do not try to overwrite the original timeline entry.

### Documents and digital 201 files

Open a saved employee and select **Documents**. Add a category, description and the
appropriate file. Supported types include PDF, images and DOCX, within the displayed
limits. Download or archive documents from the same record. Archiving preserves
history; private personnel files are available only to permitted HR staff.

### Account Manager

Only Administrators can open **Account Manager**. Employee records and sign-in
access are related but distinct: importing or creating an employee record alone
does not automatically create a sign-in account.

| Responsibility | Main scope |
| --- | --- |
| Administrator | Company and account administration, protected oversight and independent financial approval |
| HR Generalist | Personnel, contact details, time and leave, and operational HR work |
| Recruiter | Applicants, offers and onboarding |
| Learning and Performance Officer | Goals, appraisals, learning and certifications |
| Payroll Officer | Financial preparation and reporting in Workforce Payroll |
| Manager | Own work, current direct reports and specifically assigned reviews |
| Employee | Own information and requests in Workforce Employee |

Use **Manage access** to change responsibilities or enabled sign-in, with a reason.
Use **Access history** to review earlier changes. Disable sign-in when access must
stop; archive employment when appropriate while retaining historical records.
Responsibilities can be combined, but each workflow still enforces its own limits.

## Hiring and separation

### Follow an application

Recruiting staff use **Hiring** to maintain applicant information, application status,
résumé files and private recruiting notes. Keep factual dates and supporting documents
with the appropriate applicant. An application does not create an employee account.

1. Create or open the applicant and review the application details.
2. Add permitted résumé or application attachments.
3. Schedule an interview and assign the responsible interviewer.
4. Record feedback against the assigned interview. Managers see their assigned work rather than the complete private applicant record.

In the interview agenda, use **Today** or **Next 7 days** for company dates. The
seven-day window starts tomorrow. **My pending feedback** shows assigned interviews
that have started and still need your feedback.

### Offers and onboarding

Prepare an offer with the reviewed employment and pay terms. Submit it for the
required independent approval. Record delivery and acceptance or decline from
actual evidence; saving a record does not send an offer to the candidate.

Convert an accepted offer through the hiring workflow. The conversion creates the
employee record and standard onboarding tasks without duplicating an earlier
successful conversion. Review the resulting employment and contact information,
assign tasks and track their completion. Sign-in provisioning remains a separate
Administrator action.

In **Hiring → Onboarding → Task follow-ups**, review open tasks across checklists.
Use **Overdue**, **Due today** or the assignee filter, then choose **Open checklist**.
Managers use **Your onboarding tasks** to review their assignments by deadline.

### Exit and clearance

Open the employee's exit workflow, enter the actual last working date and assign
clearance tasks. Review the readiness information and complete or resolve required
items before finalizing. Keep reasons and factual completion dates in the record.

Archiving and disabling access preserve personnel and workflow history. Final pay
is handled separately in Workforce Payroll. A clearance item, account status or
employee archive must not be treated as proof that a payment has been made.

## Attendance and leave

### Review daily attendance

Open **Attendance → Daily attendance** and choose a date from the calendar. Search
by employee name or code, or choose a department. **Open shifts**, **Completed**,
**No check-in** and **On leave** count matching roster entries. Choose **All** to
clear the status filter. Use **Previous** and **Next** when the results span
multiple pages. On narrower windows, each row stacks the status, punch times
and available actions. The record-a-check-in picker is separate from roster search.
Review missing punches alongside scheduled work and approved leave.
Employees use the punch method assigned by HR, either an office station or their
own Android account.

HR can record a justified exception or past attendance for an employee with no
record. Enter the correct work date, times and reason. For an existing record,
review the current values before saving a correction. History retains the change.

### Review requests and overtime

1. Open **Attendance → Requests & approvals** and choose **Correction requests** or **Overtime approvals**.
2. Compare the proposal with recorded attendance, shift and break information.
3. Enter the review note and approve or reject only when you are an eligible reviewer.
4. Read the retained decision history. Submission alone does not change a recorded punch or establish payable overtime.

An overtime request records reviewed minutes. Payroll preparation still reviews
the relevant attendance and premium sources. A change to attendance can require
another review of the earlier overtime basis.

### Configure leave and balances

Permitted HR staff maintain leave types, holidays, workweeks, half-days, accrual,
carryover and approval steps. Use dated policy versions for changes that take
effect later. Filed requests retain their original charge dates and approval plan.

For opening balances, choose the effective date and review the existing entitlement,
usage, reservations and expiring carry. Preview entered or imported values before
posting. Use **credit adjustments** for a reviewed delta; a remaining balance is
not the same thing as a credit to add.

### Approve or cancel leave

In **Leave → Requests**, choose **Needs my review** for requests you can decide
now. You can search by employee name or code and choose **From** and **Through**
dates. A request appears if any of its dates overlap the selected range.

Review the request's charge dates, quantity, balance and approval history. Only the
current eligible reviewer can decide a step; self-approval is blocked. A request
stays pending until its required steps are complete. Cancellation requires a reason
and follows the request's date and status rules.

Administrators configure temporary substitutes through **Approval delegation**.
Delegation covers the selected operational workflow and dates; it does not grant
account administration or payroll approval.

## Office and remote work

### Set a work arrangement

HR uses **Attendance → Setup → Work arrangements** to save a dated on-site, remote or
hybrid policy with a reason. New policies are drafts until deliberately activated.
Hybrid policies name the office weekdays. An open shift keeps its saved work date
and policy for checkout. Existing punches remain unchanged.

Office restrictions require an active station with a verified photo-backed punch.
Test the station before activating a real employee's policy. Use reasoned HR
corrections for missing punches, camera failures and justified exceptions.

### Use the office station

1. An Administrator enrolls the PC under **Attendance → Setup → Office stations**. HR sets the employee's personal attendance PIN.
2. Open the kiosk and let an Administrator unlock the attendance-only session.
3. The employee enters their code and PIN, faces the webcam and selects check-in or checkout.
4. Wait for a saved receipt. Retry the same capture if delivery is uncertain.

Each punch needs a fresh photo. A failed upload does not record attendance.
Personal fields clear after a confirmed action or an idle interval. Five incorrect
PIN attempts cause a 15-minute lock. Station authorization stays in memory and
expires after 16 hours. Biometrics and facial recognition remain disabled.

Authorized HR can review private photos from Daily attendance. The station page
has a cleanup action for photos older than seven days and abandoned uploads.
Receipts and photo hashes remain after cleanup. Review storage before the
128 MiB evidence limit is reached.

### Assign and review daily work

Managers open **Attendance → Daily reports** and select **Tasks** to assign work.
Employees use **My work** in Android to update progress and submit a report.
Include completed and unfinished work, blockers, next actions and task snapshots.

Open a submission from **Needs your review** or **Daily reports**. Read the report
and prior decisions, enter a review note, then choose **Approve** or **Return for
revision**. Employees can revise and resubmit. The history retains each submission
and decision.

Use **Quick dates** to filter today's reports, the last seven days, month to date
or last month. Search by employee name or code and combine it with a status.
Each page shows up to 25 matching reports.

Reports do not change attendance, overtime, appraisals or pay. An employee can
check out without submitting a report. Use the shift start date for overnight work.

## Development and employee support

### Goals appraisals and learning

Open **Employees > Development** for Goals, Appraisals, learning records,
certifications and Career plans. The Learning and Performance responsibility
administers the combined workspace; Managers retain current-team and assigned scopes.

Define factual goals, review cycles and assigned reviewers. Eligible staff record
progress through the permitted controls. Review the goal snapshots
before submitting an appraisal. Published reviews preserve their original facts;
later progress changes do not rewrite them. Keep confidential HR notes separate
from feedback shared with the employee.

Use **Drafts** to find overdue or upcoming appraisals. Choose **Awaiting
publication** to see submitted reviews. Large lists show 25 appraisals per page.

Record training dates, progress and completion outcomes. Maintain certificate
issue and expiry dates from supporting facts. Link private evidence only through
the permitted document workflow. Use **Overdue**, **Due today** and **Next 30 days**
for open training. Certifications have corresponding expiry filters. A notice
opens the specific record. Press **Ctrl+F** to focus search. Larger training and
certification lists show 25 records per page. Career plans record development
targets and milestones. Manage salary in Workforce Payroll and job title and
department in the employee record.

### Confidential employee relations

Open **Employees > Employee relations** to maintain a confidential case. Record
the category, priority, description and follow-up dates. Add factual notes and
complete follow-ups before resolving or closing a case. Filter by **Open
follow-ups**, **Overdue** or **Due today** to find cases that need attention.

Administrators oversee cases and assign handlers. HR Generalists see their assigned
cases. A reporting relationship or Payroll role does not grant case access. Case
states are internal tracking records rather than automatic disciplinary decisions.

### Services and benefits

Use **Employee services** to route requests to the correct handling staff member,
set due dates and record outcomes. Shared messages are visible to the requester;
do not place confidential case notes in a service thread. Requesters can follow
their own requests and respond through Workforce Employee.

Filter requests by **Active**, **Overdue** or **Due today**. **Assigned to me**
focuses on your queue. HR can also choose **Unassigned**.

In **Benefits**, maintain plan guidance, record eligibility review, enroll employees
and manage permitted dependents and coverage dates. Keep the reason for each change.
An enrollment record does not itself contact an insurer or change payroll amounts.
Review any monetary effect separately in Workforce Payroll.

## Preparing payroll

Open Workforce Payroll using a current Payroll Officer or Administrator account.
Use the company's supported pay period and reviewed rule pack. Financial preparation
and independent approval are different responsibilities even when several duties
are assigned to one account.

### Set up inputs

Maintain the salary basis and recurring allowances or deductions for each employee.
Check employment dates, coverage and the evidence behind any change. Benefits
administration records do not automatically become salary or deduction items.

Review payroll holiday classifications and the applicable statutory reference
information. Premium calculations depend on the reviewed attendance, work dates,
classification and employee basis; selecting a label is not a substitute for evidence.

### Prepare a monthly register or cutoff

1. Create the period, title and planned pay date.
2. Add employees and load their current source information.
3. Review actual coverage, attendance proposals, leave, holidays, overtime, night minutes and any documented exceptions.
4. Check allowances, deductions, contributions, withholding and net pay. Missing attendance does not automatically justify a salary deduction.
5. Save each worksheet and confirm that the included and omitted employees are correct.
6. Submit the reviewed register for independent approval.

A source change can invalidate a saved review. Refresh and assess the new facts
before resubmitting. Resolve negative net pay and incomplete required inputs rather
than editing an issued payslip later.

### Other pay workflows

Use the dedicated **13th-month pay**, **Annual tax**, **Final pay** or **Amendments**
workflow for those obligations. They have their own coverage, payment-date, evidence
and independent-review controls. A general allowance or a second register must
not be used to duplicate a source already handled by one of those workflows.

## Approval payments and corrections

### Independent review

An eligible Administrator opens the submitted worksheet, checks source freshness,
coverage, exceptions and totals, and either returns it with a reason or approves
it. The approver must satisfy the workflow's creator, preparer and submitter
exclusions. Do not reuse another person's account to bypass those checks.

Approval creates an immutable issued statement. The statement can then appear in
the recipient's own payslip view. Approval is not a payment receipt.

### Record a payment

1. Open the approved run or statement and select the intended recipient.
2. Check the exact amount and whether an earlier receipt already exists.
3. An Administrator records the actual payment date and reference from evidence.
4. Confirm the recipient's receipt and the outstanding status of other recipients.

Recording payment does not transfer money. One person's receipt does not mark a
coworker paid. The run remains partly outstanding until its required recipients
have individual receipts. Do not infer a person's receipt date from batch status.

### Correct issued pay

Use **Amendments** for a controlled linked adjustment to an eligible original
statement. Review the original, the corrected difference and receipt-year treatment.
Submit the supplement for independent approval, then record its own settlement.
The original payslip and its receipt remain unchanged.

An unpaid statement may have a reasoned withdrawal route when its workflow permits
rescheduling. A completed payment is not an editable draft or an automatic reversal.

### Final pay and tax advice

Review service end, payment date, included unpaid sources and tax advice separately.
Previously settled sources must not be counted again as new cash. Follow explicit
cross-year controls where separation and payment fall in different years. Approved
advice, employee archiving and clearance completion do not establish payment.

## Reports files and government review

### HR reports

In Workforce HR, open **Reports**, choose the period and permitted filters, and
generate the preview. **Last month** selects the full previous calendar month.
Review attendance and leave summaries, headcount groups or
workforce trends. Save or select custom columns where available, then export a
new CSV or Excel file.

Administrators can open **Reports > Leadership** for aggregate oversight. The
report describes recorded facts and current workflow states. Missing attendance
is not inferred absence, and current group counts do not reconstruct past headcount.

### Payroll and government records

Workforce Payroll provides financial reports, individual payment history and
**Government** review. Choose the agency and coverage or receipt period as required.
Review registration details, original pay sources, contribution allocations,
unknown amounts and recorded remittances before generating a worksheet.

An internal CSV, Excel worksheet, reference draft or passing file preflight is not
an official return or proof of validator acceptance. A remittance entry records an
evidenced payment; it does not initiate one. Preserve unresolved information rather
than replacing it with zero to force a complete total.

### Import files

1. Download the template from the corresponding employee, opening-balance, attendance or leave import screen.
2. Preserve its headers and keep codes and phone numbers as text.
3. Select the file and review every preview error using the original row numbers.
4. Correct the source and preview again before posting.
5. Check the posted result and retained batch history before trying another submission.

Attendance imports create correction requests, and leave imports create requests
subject to normal review. An imported request is not an approved record. Employee
imports do not create sign-in accounts. Formulas, macros, external connections and
unsupported worksheet structures are rejected.

## Workforce Employee for Android

Sign in with your company account. Your Home screen summarizes your own workday,
employment details and latest published pay information. Staff accounts receive
this same own-only view.

### Attendance and leave

Use **Check in** when starting and **Check out** when finishing. Routine punches
use company server time. Use the saved break controls for the shift. For a missing
or incorrect punch, open **Attendance**, choose the work date and submit a correction
with the proposed times and reason. Request overtime from the corresponding form.
Proposals remain separate from recorded attendance until an eligible review.

In **Leave**, choose the leave type, dates and full-day or half-day option. Review
the displayed charge and balance before submitting. Follow the approval history
and use cancellation only where the saved status and dates allow it.

### Payslips and service requests

Open **Payslips** to review your published statements and recorded payment status.
Use the available export or print action to save a deliberate personal copy.
An issued statement and a payment receipt are different facts.

Use **Services** to request employment documents, information updates, benefits
support or general HR help. Read the shared responses and reply in the same thread.
Submitting a requested change does not automatically edit your employment record.

### Profile and alerts

Open **Profile** from your circular profile control. Employment fields are read-only;
contact HR for corrections. You can update permitted contact and emergency details
and choose a profile photo. Review account, password and session controls carefully.

Open the notification Inbox for personal reminders. In **Device alerts**, select
**Enable alerts**, allow Android's notification permission and use **Send test alert**.
Alerts contain a generic hint; sign in to read private details. Android power and
notification settings can affect reception. **Sign out** or **Disable alerts** turns
off device reception; closing the app retains an existing alert opt-in.

## Settings and troubleshooting

### Company and personal settings

Administrators maintain the company name, logo, time zone and permitted defaults
in **Settings**. Optional workspace visibility changes presentation, not server
permissions or historical records. Assign responsibilities through Account Manager.

Use display preferences for desktop light or dark appearance and supported scaling.
Android follows device appearance. Profile photos and company logos serve different
purposes: the profile identifies the person, while company branding identifies
the deployment.

Save your work before installing a company update. Keep each application on its
own update path and use only a package supplied for that company. A source-code
download is not a configured installer.

### Common situations

| Situation | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A workspace is missing | Check your assigned responsibility and the company's optional-section settings with an Administrator. |
| Desktop sign-in is refused | Confirm that staff access is enabled. Ordinary Employees use Workforce Employee. |
| A record changed while you were editing | Refresh, compare the saved values and review your draft before retrying. |
| A save has an uncertain result | Use the offered status check or exact retry. Avoid creating another copy until the first result is known. |
| A period or balance is unavailable | Check dates, current policy, coverage and required source setup rather than entering a guessed value. |
| An alert does not arrive | Check alert opt-in, Android permission, power settings and the in-app Inbox. |

### Keep the audit trail

Use reasons, retained history, controlled cancellation and linked adjustments.
Do not replace missing facts with invented dates or amounts. Keep sensitive
exports in company-approved locations and close an app when finished. Biometric
device connection controls are visible and disabled.
