# Desktop0.1.46 and local portal0.1.6 — notification checks

This increment adds free in-app notices. It sends no email/push, transfers no
money and files no government return. Older installed clients retain their inbox
contract. Portal0.1.5 can remain running until its drafts are saved.

1. Open Notifications after sign-in. Check the Payroll, Appraisals and Learning
   filters; refresh and mark a notice read/unread. Resetting read history changes
   only the inbox. It does not approve or edit the source record.
2. Payroll notices distinguish issued advice from an individual recorded payment.
   Open record should select the correct payroll module and own statement. A
   source settled through final pay must not suggest a second cash payment.
3. Appraisal reviewers receive current assigned deadlines. HR publication tasks
   exclude their own review. Published-review notices contain no rating/HR notes.
4. Planned/in-progress training has upcoming/due/overdue reminders; valid active
   certificates have expiry reminders. Complete/cancel/reassign a synthetic item
   and refresh to confirm an obsolete task disappears.
5. A Manager sees only current permitted own/team tasks. Their portal shows own
   notices only. Disabling access prevents further reads with the same session.
6. In the separate local portal0.1.6, monthly payroll notices open My payslips.
   Other own advice/development notices remain informational until their portal
   record views exist. No misleading Open record button is shown for those.

Current portal/browser/phone visuals and actual email confirmation remain
unverified because the supported UI runtimes cannot initialize and a no-charge
approved mail setup is unresolved. Exact government-file acceptance also remains
partial. Paid-dependent features stay on hold. These limits do not prevent this
separate in-app increment from being checked and delivered.
