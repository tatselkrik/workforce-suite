# Organization chart

Open **Employees → Organization chart** beside Directory. The chart uses saved **Employment → Reports to** relationships. Click an employee card to open the existing employee record; **Back to chart** or closing/saving the record returns to the chart. Editing Reports to and saving rebuilds the hierarchy from the refreshed employee records. The view does not change reporting relationships itself.

Cards show name, position, department, work location and employee code. Team arrows expand/collapse direct reports; **Expand all** and **Collapse all** apply to the current filtered hierarchy. Missing names for employment fields are shown as not set. Employees without an assigned manager are separate top-level cards; no CEO or manager is inferred from their title or access role.

Search by employee name, code or position, and filter by department or location. **Department not set** and **Location not set** are distinct from all values. Inactive location assignments retain their current label. Managers above matching employees remain as **Reporting context** so a filter does not invent a reporting relationship. Unmatched siblings and unrelated teams are hidden. A filter change expands matches; **Clear filters** returns to the active chart. Counts describe matching employees and retained context within the current access scope, including branches that are collapsed.

HR Generalists and Administrators can view the company employee hierarchy. **Include archived** adds retained historical employee records to their view; the default shows active employees. A Manager sees only their own record and active direct reports that are available under existing database permissions. Manager filters and counts cannot reveal indirect reports or unrelated employees. Contact details, emergency information and personnel files do not appear in cards. Opening a record preserves existing record permissions; a Manager does not gain employee editing or HR-only documents/history. Ordinary Employee accounts still use the future portal and cannot enter the desktop.

**Manager outside this view** means a saved reporting manager is unavailable under the current status/access scope. The app does not fetch or infer that person's hidden record. Existing database rules reject self-reporting, cycles, invalid managers and archiving an active manager with active reports. A defensive read-only fallback displays a malformed legacy cycle separately for HR review rather than looping or dropping people. It does not alter the saved relationship. Deep hierarchies cap visual indentation and show their actual level so cards remain readable.

The chart is computed in memory from the existing authorized employee snapshot. Refresh reloads saved changes from Supabase; there is no new table, RPC, permission grant, dependency or database migration. Chart filters/expanded state are not persisted and are cleared on sign-out or access changes. No chart export, drag-to-reassign, future-dated organization, dotted-line reporting or employee portal is implemented.
