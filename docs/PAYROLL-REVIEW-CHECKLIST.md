# Payroll review checklist — 0.1.33

Open **Payroll** after updating. Administrators and HR see the full workspace;
Managers see **My payslips**, containing only their own approved records.

1. **Salary profiles:** choose an employee, enter an effective month and monthly
   or daily pay. Review the rate/divisor, statutory coverage, fixed PhilHealth
   basic and recurring components. Save with a reason; reopen and check history.
2. **Holiday calendar:** classify regular, special nonworking and special working
   holidays, including local holidays. Review rest-day treatment. This calendar
   does not rewrite previously filed leave charges.
3. **Monthly register:** create a completed 2026 month from February onward and
   add the intended employees. Load attendance proposals, then review actual
   regular/overtime/night minutes, breaks, paid leave, eligible unworked holidays
   and explicit unpaid time. Missing check-ins do not automatically deduct pay.
4. **Allowances and deductions:** add a taxable allowance, rice/laundry benefit,
   reimbursement or documented deduction/advance. Check employee contributions,
   withholding, employer costs and net pay. Employer-benefit memos do not add to
   take-home pay. Save the reviewed worksheet with its explanation.
5. **Changed source:** edit a salary or attendance source before submission.
   Submission should require **Refresh source** and another worksheet review;
   issued slips should retain their original facts and values.
6. **Approval:** verify included and omitted employees, then submit. A different
   Administrator approves; the submitter cannot approve their own register.
   HR can submit for an Administrator, or a second authorized Administrator can
   review. Returned registers can be corrected and resubmitted with history.
7. **Payslips:** open an approved slip, export PDF and CSV, and compare totals to
   the worksheet. Private HR review notes and deduction consent references should
   not appear in employee-facing slips. Managers must not see team salaries.
8. **Reports and payment:** export the register, statutory worksheet and annual
   recorded-month summary. Record a payment date/reference only for an actual
   verified payment. This records history; it does not send money or file returns.

The **Rules and limits** view links the supported Philippine rules. The initial
pack supports completed 2026 months beginning February. Interim payroll payments
can be recorded as advances when reconciling a month; actual cutoff disbursement
workflows remain separate. Annual tax adjustment, prior-employer balances, final
pay and actual 13th-month payouts are not automatically calculated. Review these
with the company's payroll practitioner before workplace use.
