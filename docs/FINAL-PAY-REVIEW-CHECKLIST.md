# Final-pay review checklist
Use Payroll → Final pay after installing the private update and signing in.
Use fictional employees for new reviews. Recording settlement records an actual
payment already made; it does not transfer money.

- [ ] The Final pay tab shows reviews for Payroll Officers and Administrators and only own published
  statements for Managers. Search finds employee names/codes and status labels remain clear.
- [ ] New review lets HR select an employee, last working day and planned cash
  date. Archived employees remain payable. A missing exit case or clearance
  completion does not prevent a review.
- [ ] The worksheet shows outstanding issued salary/cutoff and earned13th-month
  sources. Compare the combined cash with original nets: the final settlement
  offsets those original balances rather than paying each net a second time.
- [ ] Review vacation conversion and prior conversions, cause-specific separation,
  retirement coverage and eligibility, extra amounts, contribution adjustments
  and prospective annual withholding/refund. Private evidence is visible to HR.
- [ ] Required review confirmations and reasons are clear; invalid/incomplete
  reviews cannot be submitted. An overdue planned payment displays a warning.
- [ ] Submitted review is read-only for the independent Administrator. Creator,
  worksheet preparer and submitter cannot approve their own case.
- [ ] An approved statement remains available as PDF/CSV. It excludes HR evidence,
  birth details, private payment references and raw source bundles.
- [ ] An unpaid approved statement can be withdrawn with a reason and explicit
  unpaid confirmation. Its immutable old statement and withdrawal status remain.
- [ ] Settlement confirmation states the exact combined cash. The source salary/
  13th balances then show settled through final pay, and coworkers stay outstanding.
- [ ] Final-pay lists, worksheet footer and statement remain aligned in Light/Dark
  and enlarged display scale.
- [ ] Your installed version appears at the top right; the new-version banner
  persists after sign-in until the update is installed.
