# Account Manager0.1.63 testing checklist

Candidate only: packaging, private publication, activation and actual installation
still require their separate delivery evidence. Installed0.1.62 does not expose
the complete specialist contract.

After verified delivery, use fictional employees/accounts for these checks:

- Account Manager appears only for an Administrator.
- Create a new employee and staff sign-in together. Employment, contact and emergency
  details belong to the same person; no separate employee linking step appears.
- Add sign-in access from an existing employee. The employee keeps their identity,
  employment record and history. Existing sign-in identities keep their password.
- Choose one or several compatible staff duties. Learning and Performance is one
  role. Employee alone is portal-only. Duplicate/unsupported role sets are refused.
- HR Generalist handles personnel/attendance/leave without automatically receiving
  Recruiting, Development or Payroll rights. Explicit combined duties remain available.
- Recruiter handles hiring; Learning & Performance Officer handles Development;
  Payroll Officer prepares payroll. Their unrelated private HR data stays restricted.
- Manager access follows actual reporting and assignments. A reporting link alone
  grants no Manager role. Combined specialist/Manager duties retain both scopes.
- Payroll approval, payment and remittance recording remain independent Administrator
  actions. Preparing or submitting your own financial work does not approve it.
- Edit roles or disable sign-in with a reason. Refresh the other test session and
  verify the changed access applies, even with its existing sign-in token.
- Owner/self/last-Administrator safeguards and established employee identity locks
  reject unsafe changes. Retain employee/history records when access is disabled.
- Open Access history. Review role and access events, revisions, reasons and current
  actor labels. Older unknown facts remain explicitly unknown.
- Refresh history while editing roles: unsaved choices remain. Changed source data
  requires a fresh review. Another account's history cannot be substituted.
- For an interrupted setup, retry the same attempt or review its pending status.
  A linked replacement/cancellation keeps the original history and reserved identity.
  No retry silently resets a password, deletes Auth or sends duplicate email.
- Send confirmation remains explicit; actual email delivery is deferred for now.
- Open your own Profile, notices and published statements; their own-record scope
  remains. Ordinary Employees continue to use the separate local portal.
- The signed updater is available to enabled desktop staff. Employees, disabled
  accounts and anonymous callers cannot use the desktop update channel.

Local permission/transaction, geometry and hosted checks are recorded separately.
Headless layout checks do not establish the appearance on Kirk's installed screen.
No paid service, hardware, public portal, new payroll rule or wider workspace
reorganization is part of this release.
