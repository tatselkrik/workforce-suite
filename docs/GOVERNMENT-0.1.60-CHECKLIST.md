# 0.1.60 — what to check

Sign in as Administrator or HR Generalist. Open **Payroll → Government → BIR →
Annual recorded sources** and choose a receipt year, then **Generate / refresh**.

- Open **Recorded pay details** on an issued source. Monthly/cutoff sources
  show basic pay, paid leave, holiday pay, premiums, overtime, night differential,
  allowances and employee contributions. Labels and amounts should align at
  normal and smaller window sizes, in light/dark themes and larger display text.
- Check **13th-month payout**: components describe the current payout. Annual
  earned basic, entitlement and earlier payouts are not displayed as new pay.
- Check final-pay components: original contained pay is excluded, and returned
  deposits are named separately. The additional settlement total includes those
  deposits; it is not automatically taxable gross. Component fields overlap.
- Annual tax settlements and review-only amendments show why they add no new
  pay components. Original service dates and actual receipt dates remain visible.
- Unpaid, missing-receipt, postdated and withdrawn sources retain their receipt
  treatment. A displayed component is not proof of received income. Missing
  historical amounts say **Unknown**, rather than inventing zero.
- Choose CSV or Excel and **Export fresh pay details**. The separate internal
  worksheet has one row per source; amounts keep exact cents. Blank component
  columns are not applicable, while `Unknown` means missing historical data.
  Screen filters do not limit the fresh export. Existing files cannot be replaced.
- **Export fresh annual worksheet** retains the original 18-column withholding
  format. Refresh afterward to load pay details again.
- Managers and ordinary Employees cannot use this HR/Admin-only government
  review. No current salary, private HR evidence or tax identifier is added to
  these pay-detail exports.

The company’s recorded data may not contain every example. Fictional local
checks exercise all six source kinds and missing/unknown cases. These are
internal recorded facts, not expected BIR values, certified 2316 fields, an
officially accepted agency file, a remittance or a government filing.
The manual BIR module check and separate portal-browser reminder remain deferred.
