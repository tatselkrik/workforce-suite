# 13th-month pay — review checklist

1. Open **Payroll → 13th-month pay**. Create a completed2026 earned-coverage run with a planned pay date and add a synthetic employee.
2. Open the worksheet. Check included issued basic; fill each missing opening interval with verified current-employer basic or an evidenced zero. Overlaps, missing coverage and future/other-employer basic must not pass as complete earned records.
3. Review actual coverage/service independently of the account role. Document basic corrections or employer-funded policy top-ups/advances. Confirm earlier in-app installments are automatic and not entered again as external payments.
4. Review the shared90k benefit cap, other/prior-employer benefit facts, explicitly reviewed supplementary/cumulative withholding and month-level contributions. Review checkboxes/notes are required even for zero; automatic annual tax reconciliation follows separately.
5. Save, confirm included/omitted staff and submit. Self-approval must fail. Another Administrator can open **Review saved worksheet** to inspect HR evidence, then approve immutable slips.
6. Create a concurrent/later installment. Approving an earlier one must invalidate its old source and prevent double payment. Refresh resets all three reviews; the open form retains unsaved edits. New worksheets seed protected prior benefit totals.
7. Export the register and own benefit slip CSV/PDF. Check exact year/as-of/pay date, amounts and privacy. A Manager sees only their own issued slip. Archived employee eligibility stays separate from account sign-in access.
8. Record a synthetic or independently verified actual paid date/reference. No money moves; issued slips and earlier history remain unchanged.

Review December24 timing and any employer-funded advance. Workforce Employee displays own issued benefit slips. This module does not file returns, transfer money, confirm remittances or automatically annualize tax.
