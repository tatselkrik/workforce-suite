# Final pay — native worksheet and protected settlement

Open **Final pay** in Workforce Payroll. See [amendments and receipt-year rules](PAYROLL-AMENDMENTS.md) for corrections and cross-year treatment. This module records reviewed obligations and settlements; it does not transfer money, file returns or issue an official2316.

## Reviewed scope and dates

Prepare the last actual salary/cutoff and earned13th-month slips using the existing workflows. A final review includes every outstanding issued slip whose completed period falls on or before the planned cash date. Original basic, premiums, contributions and withholding remain immutable. Review partial service and any source period extending beyond the last working day before issuing that source; this module does not project salary or create a second13th-month entitlement. Missing attendance does not imply an absence deduction.

The original pack supported private-sector same-year2026 separation and cash dates from6January2026. Source0.1.41 also retains earlier separation dates with explicit receipt-year evidence and reviewed2026 cash dates, without invented service coverage. Government/special occupational retirement and later cash years still need separate rule review and implementation. The existing individual-payment and annual-tax workflows retain their documented scope.

The actual employment start must be recorded. Archived employees may be paid. Clearance, account access, employee archiving and an active exit case are not payment gates. The default release deadline is30days from separation; a more favorable company deadline may be shorter. An overdue planned release is flagged rather than refused. Settlement uses the approved cash date, no later than today and no earlier than approval. Withdraw an unpaid statement and prepare a fresh dated review when payment is rescheduled.

## Entitlements and tax classification

- Review payable private vacation credits, the daily rate and all vacation days already monetized in the cash year. Up to12days use the de-minimis exclusion; excess joins the same annualPHP90,000 benefit pool as13th-month pay. Prior recorded final conversions set a protected floor. SIL, sick leave and generic company balances are not automatically vacation credits or automatically cashable; record other owed amounts with evidenced classification.
- Cause-specific separation minima use latest monthly basic **plus regular allowances**. Half-month causes retain at least one month's pay; redundancy/labor-saving devices use a month per credited service year. Service includes the last working day and uses calendar anniversaries; a six-month fraction rounds the monetary multiplier. Review the total cumulative award and earlier award payments separately.
- Ordinary statutory retirement requires evidenced coverage, age60 or later and actual five-year service. Its minimum is22.5daily basic units per credited year; apply a more favorable evidenced plan/policy amount. Monetary rounding does not grant five-year eligibility. Daily basic excludes COLA/allowances unless a more favorable agreement applies.
- Retirement defaults taxable. The no-plan statutory tax route requires an eligible employee who is **not covered** by an employer retirement plan. An employee covered but ineligible under a plan cannot automatically fall back to that exemption. A qualified-plan route requires reviewed BIR qualification, actual plan coverage, age50, actual ten-year service and first qualifying availment. Plan terms and legal eligibility require HR evidence; account roles never determine them.
- Separation exemption is an explicit evidenced review for qualifying involuntary/death/disability causes. Resignation, just cause and generic company-policy awards are not automatically exempt. Extra taxable earnings, shared benefits, deposit returns and lawful deductions have separate classifications and private evidence. Labels appear on the own statement; evidence references do not. Earlier award overpayments block issue without automatic wage recovery.

Additional employee SSS/PhilHealth/Pag-IBIG are reviewed adjustments only, distinct from contributions already removed in source slips. Lawful non-tax deductions reduce available cash, not taxable compensation. Negative automatic employee debt is not created.

## Prospective annual tax and payment meaning

Annualization includes received pay plus included unpaid salary/13th-month income and new taxable/shared amounts at the approved cash date. Retain evidenced nonoverlapping openings, net prior-employer certificates, shared-benefit floors and earlier settled adjustments. Every missing current-employer interval needs evidence, including zero income. The engine credits original withholding once, calculates the signed final adjustment, adds a refund to cash or collects additional withholding within available cash. A deficiency beyond collectible cash remains separate employer handling, with a required settlement reference; it is not an automatic employee debt.

The **combined final cash** is the actual employee cash amount. Source-net receipts with origin `final_settlement` discharge their original immutable balances through that combined cash and statutory offsets; they are not separate full-net cash transfers. Each is bound to its protected settled case, employee, source and cash date. Future annual sources include final additional income and the signed settled tax credit exactly once. Future13th-month worksheets reserve settled final shared benefits without inventing external opening income.

## Approval, freshness and history

Payroll Officers and Administrators prepare, save, refresh and submit. An Administrator distinct from the creator, most recent worksheet preparer and submitter approves. Protected writers recheck current membership/revision under the company payroll lock7 and verify the saved source. A coworker's payment can advance a batch revision without invalidating this person's immutable facts. A changed own payment, income, employment fact or earlier tax close requires fresh review.

Approval issues an immutable aggregate own statement. Reserved source slips cannot be recorded through a separate individual or legacy batch payment while that statement awaits settlement. Another annual tax close cannot duplicate its pending adjustment. An Administrator may withdraw an **unpaid** statement with explicit confirmation; the original statement and own withdrawal status remain immutable, and a new review can use fresh facts. Recorded settlements cannot be reversed or edited in this increment; amendments and receipt reversals are separate future work.

Settlement atomically records the full combined cash/tax-handling attestations, discharges only included source balances, updates affected batch summaries, appends aggregate own settlement status and retains private audit evidence. A failed receipt/status/audit write rolls the transaction back. Exact actor-bound creation, settlement and withdrawal retries are safe. A later legacy batch action records only remaining coworkers. Overlapping employment cannot receive a second issued final settlement.

Cases, private payment references, birth details and raw evidence are protected Payroll preparation. Other staff read/export only their own immutable statements and aggregate settled/withdrawn status. Ordinary Employee accounts still cannot sign into the desktop. PrintablePDF and UTF-8CSV exclude HR notes/evidence, and HR registers use current access. Private drafts and published statements have separate lifecycles.
