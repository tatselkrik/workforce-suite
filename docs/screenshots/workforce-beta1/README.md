# Workforce Suite beta1 screenshots

Captured and visually reviewed on30October2026 for1.0.0-beta.1. These are actual
application renders with fictional data, rather than image-generator concepts.
They do not show company records or establish authenticated production acceptance.

excluded from the installed release executables. The employee images use the owned
Android emulator and a mock own-record fixture, with backend authentication and
writes disabled. The final signed release was verified separately; instrumentation
and preview fixtures are excluded from its APK. Compact views may scroll normally.

The HR overview, employee directory, Payroll monthly register/settings, employee
home/pay/profile and sign-in screens were inspected before being copied here.
CAPTURES.json records hashes of the unmodified, reviewed PNGs. Fictional names,
amounts, dates and company branding are interface examples, not business claims.
The retained PC browser portal has its own lifecycle and is not pictured here.
