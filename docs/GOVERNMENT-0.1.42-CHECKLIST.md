# Version0.1.42 review checklist

Use fictional records and synthetic receipts. Check the release evidence for the
exact installed version. No action here files a return or sends money.

1. Open **Payroll → Government** as HR/Admin. Select month and SSS, PhilHealth,
   Pag-IBIG or BIR, then **Generate / refresh**. Known shares and any unresolved
   coverage/receipt issues should remain distinct from the complete liability.
2. Find an employee/source. Salary/cutoff contributions use their earned month;
   BIR follows the individual's actual received month. Unpaid approvals remain
   excluded from received withholding. Do not substitute a whole batch's paid date.
3. Open **Review coverage / shares** on a special contribution source. Add unique
   original covered months, explicit employer/SSS EC shares, supporting reference
   and reason. Employee shares must total the unchanged issued amount. Save/reopen.
4. Submit the draft with its evidence review. A different Administrator approves.
   All previous editors/submitters and the source employee are excluded. Check
   **Review history**. Revise an approved review through a linked successor.
5. As Administrator, **Record remittance** using a fictional evidenced receipt.
   Whole receipt amount must equal its covered-month shares. Check whole receipt
   versus selected-month amounts and that HR Generalists cannot record it.
6. **Void incorrect record**, retaining the reason/history. Use **Correct / replace**
   on the final voided entry. Originals remain visible; repeated identical entry
   must not duplicate a payment. End synthetic tests with their receipts voided.
7. **Export fresh review CSV**. Compare selected-month summary and inclusion flags.
   Voided receipts contribute zero. Complete unresolved totals must not appear as
   zero. Private references, evidence and review notes must not appear in the CSV.
8. A Manager must not see Government or read its protected tables/API. Sign out
   and verify no report or form from the previous account remains accessible.
