# Payment records — review checklist

1. Open Payroll → Payments. Search an employee and switch between Outstanding and All issued payslips. Monthly, cutoff and13th-month sources should show their exact issued net and period.
2. As an Administrator, open one outstanding employee payment. The amount is read-only. A date, private reference, reason and confirmation are required. Save only after an actual payment; this prototype never sends money.
3. Confirm that the person's actual date appears and coworkers remain outstanding. When another recipient was already paid, the older batch-payment action records only the remaining recipients.
4. Record the final outstanding person. The run should become paid without changing an earlier receipt date or issued payslip.
5. Open an annual-tax draft and refresh its source after a payment change. It should use each person's received date and reset source reviews. Issued advice must remain unchanged.
6. HR Generalists may review records but cannot record payments. Managers must not see the Payments tab or private references. Ordinary employees remain excluded from desktop sign-in.

Individual records cover a complete issued net, not partial installments. Final-pay entitlements and final settlement are still being developed separately. An incorrect recorded payment cannot be silently overwritten.
